# Cleaning & Home Services App — Project Specification

## 1. App Overview / Theme

A location-based, on-demand home services marketplace app — starting with **cleaning services only**, built to expand into additional service categories (e.g. plumbing, electrician, pest control) in future phases without a full re-architecture.

**Core idea:** Customers book a cleaning service from an admin-managed service list. Maids (service providers) register inside the same user-facing app, get matched to nearby jobs based on a selectable service radius, and get paid per completed service.

**Design philosophy:** Keep the UI simple and minimal — this is a utility app used quickly and often (target: 500+ users/day), not a browsing/discovery app. Prioritize speed of booking over visual complexity.

---

## 2. System Architecture — Three Parts

| Component | Users | Framework |
|---|---|---|
| **User App** | Customers + Maids (same app, different roles) | React Native (mobile only, Android + iOS) |
| **Admin Panel** | Internal admin/ops team | Next.js (web dashboard) |
| **Backend API** | Serves both apps | Node.js (Express or NestJS) |

**Database:** PostgreSQL + PostGIS extension (for free radius/location-based matching)
**Auth:** Firebase Authentication (Phone/OTP)
**Notifications:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
**Payments:** Razorpay
**Hosting:** Hostinger VPS
**Geocoding:** Nominatim / OpenStreetMap (free alternative to Google Maps)

---

## 3. Core User Roles

1. **Customer** — books services, pays advance, tracks job, rates maid
2. **Maid / Service Provider** — registers, completes KYC + training, goes online/offline, accepts/rejects jobs, executes job, earns money
3. **Admin** — manages service list, approves KYC, manually assigns jobs, views payouts, handles support

---

## 4. Feature Breakdown

### 4.1 Customer Side (User App)
- Mobile OTP login (Firebase Auth)
- Browse service list (admin-managed, cleaning-only at launch)
- Select date + time slot for booking
- Pay advance via Razorpay
- Track assigned maid, job status
- In-app chat + masked calling with assigned maid
- Rate & review after job completion
- Notifications: booking confirmed, maid assigned, job started/completed, offers

### 4.2 Maid Side (User App — same app, maid role)
1. **Authentication** — Mobile OTP login, secure session, logout from all devices
2. **Worker Registration** — personal details, photo, Aadhaar (collect & store only, no UIDAI verification), address, bank account, emergency contact, nominee
3. **KYC Verification** — admin approval workflow, document status tracking, rejection reason + re-upload flow
4. **Health & Safety** — health declaration, allergies, existing conditions, blood group, emergency contact, future insurance eligibility field
5. **Training** — mandatory SOP videos, safety training, customer etiquette training, assessment quiz required before account activation
6. **Availability** — online/offline toggle, working hours, service radius selection (location-based)
7. **Job Management** — receive job requests, accept/reject within a timer window, view job details, in-app navigation, OTP-based job start
8. **Job Execution** — before photos, task checklist, in-app chat, masked calling with customer, after photos, completion confirmation
9. **Earnings** — daily/weekly/monthly earnings breakdown, commission deducted, payout history, incentive tracking
10. **Ratings** — customer ratings, feedback, performance score
11. **Support** — support tickets, FAQs, emergency SOS button
12. **Notifications** — new job requests, payout confirmations, offers, policy updates
13. **Documents** — worker agreement, privacy policy, terms acceptance (in-app e-signature/checkbox flow)
14. **Security** — encrypted document storage, role-based access, audit logs, no personal number exposure (masked calling only)

### 4.3 Admin Panel
- Manage service list (add/edit/remove services, pricing)
- View & manage all bookings
- Manual job assignment (override automation)
- KYC review queue — approve/reject with reason
- Maid management — view profiles, documents, training status, activate/deactivate
- Payout management — view earnings, process payouts
- Support ticket management
- Push manual notifications
- Audit logs / access control (role-based: super admin, ops staff, support staff)
- Basic reporting (bookings/day, revenue, active maids)

---

## 5. Key Business Logic Rules

- **Job assignment:** Sequential, not broadcast. Job goes to maid A first → if maid A rejects or times out → goes to maid B → then C, cycling through maids within the customer's location radius. Admin can also manually assign at any point.
- **Maid radius:** Each maid selects their own service radius during registration/availability setup — determines which jobs they're eligible to receive.
- **Payment:** Customer pays an advance at booking time via Razorpay. Maid earns based on number of completed services (not flat salary).
- **Activation gate:** A maid cannot go "online" / receive jobs until: KYC approved + training completed + quiz passed.
- **Aadhaar:** Collected and stored (encrypted, masked in UI) — no live government verification (UIDAI API) required for now.

## 9. Database — Core Tables (PostgreSQL + PostGIS)

- `customers` (id, name, phone, location_point [geography])
- `maids` (id, name, phone, aadhaar_doc_url, bank_details, kyc_status, training_status, service_radius_km, location_point [geography], is_online)
- `services` (id, name, description, base_price, category)
- `bookings` (id, customer_id, service_id, slot_datetime, status, advance_paid, assigned_maid_id)
- `job_assignments` (id, booking_id, maid_id, status, sequence_order, responded_at) — tracks the sequential assignment attempts
- `payments` (id, booking_id, razorpay_payment_id, amount, status)
- `earnings` (id, maid_id, booking_id, amount, commission, payout_status)
- `ratings` (id, booking_id, customer_id, maid_id, score, feedback)
- `kyc_documents` (id, maid_id, doc_type, doc_url, status, rejection_reason)
- `training_records` (id, maid_id, module, completed_at, quiz_score)
- `support_tickets` (id, user_id, user_type, subject, status)
- `audit_logs` (id, admin_id, action, target_table, target_id, timestamp)

6. Payment integration (Razorpay)
7. Job execution flow (photos, checklist, OTP start, completion)
8. Earnings + payouts
